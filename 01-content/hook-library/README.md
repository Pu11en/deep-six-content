# Educational hook library

Keep a small library of observed educational openings and Drew's feedback so each writing round has specific examples to learn from.

Refresh on Saturday nights once scheduling is enabled. For now this is a proposed storage format; no weekly job runs and no reviewed example cards have been added here. The existing research notes contain leads, not a verified performance library.

## One card per studied video

Record the original URL, creator, platform, publication date and checked date; a short opening excerpt with timestamp; the opening action actually observed; the question or promise; and where the lesson delivers its answer. Record available views/engagement with the observation date and any relevant comparison to the creator's other similar videos. Mark missing audio, visuals or metrics explicitly.

Describe the reusable structure in original words. Link the observation to a possible homeowner lesson without treating the video's claims as pest facts. Do not copy a creator's script.

Keep these distinctions:

- **Observed:** opening and payoff inspected; metrics may be unavailable.
- **Promising:** available performance suggests a useful example. Views alone cannot isolate the hook's effect.
- **Drew-approved:** an approved narration demonstrates the preferred wording and rhythm. A hook selection alone is provisional.
- **Tested on Deep Six:** our published video's results are recorded. This is still not proof that its hook alone caused performance.

Useful examples remain across refreshes. Keep concise feedback; never store rejected narration bodies. The writer receives relevant cards, while Drew's first screen remains only five hooks.
