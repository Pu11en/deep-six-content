## What a resource card contains

Source ID; original link; publisher; publication/revision and checked dates; pest/species; region; one homeowner question; supported facts with page or section references; a practical takeaway; limits; and prior-use status.

Facts and citations stay attached to the selected hook and all five narration options. A new URL alone does not make a repeated lesson new. The [source log](source-log.md) remains the record of actual content use.

## Research filter

Prefer university extension and public pest-information sources. Choose a concrete household problem that can be explained visually. Read the actual article and relevant section before drafting. Product-specific advice needs current label support; an old article’s product recommendation is not enough by itself.

This is a starting library, not five new script assignments. Source selection for the next batch is still open.
