## 1. Research stays in the background

- **At each writing run:** read trusted pest resources and prepare five different lessons from five unused sources. Check exact passages, species, region and dates against the source-use log.
- **Saturday nights, once scheduled:** study educational openings and update the [hook library](hook-library/README.md). Keep original links, observed openings, available performance and your feedback.

Articles supply facts; the library supplies opening patterns. Its examples are candidates until tested. A popular video does not prove its hook caused success. Crawler integration waits for Drew's notice that the other agent's update is ready. For now the existing subscription search and direct page/PDF checks continue.

## 2. Choose the writing

1. Show **five source choices directly in chat**, each with a link and normally one or two sentences describing its lesson, at most four. You choose one or skip for five new sources.
2. Write and show **five numbered hooks for the selected source**. You choose one or skip for five new openings using that same source. A second hook may wait in the queue with its source.
3. Show **five full narrations**, each beginning with the exact chosen hook and using the same source.
4. You choose one or skip for another five with that same hook and source.
5. Save the approved script and its evidence. Keep earlier approved scripts. End this writing run; start another when requested.

Source picks/skips guide future research; hook picks/skips guide future openings. A skip alone does not say why. Your comments carry more weight, and later corrections take precedence. The latest twelve decisions of each kind are supplied to the relevant stage; all decisions remain saved locally. This supports adaptation, not a guarantee that each batch is better.

## 3. Videos come later

Choose a saved narration when ready for production. Then: **scene plan → voiceover and measured timing → code cartoon animation → captions and sound → reusable branded outro → final review → export.**

Plan the characters’ actions around the lesson. Time the animation to the actual voiceover. Make the outro once, approve it, and reuse it; AI image/video generation can be used for that asset. Every produced preview and final video stays **9:16 in Deep Six colors**.
