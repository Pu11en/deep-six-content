**Drew selected option 2, Educational sign-off, by replying "2".** The [approved specification](APPROVED-OUTRO.md) now confirms its exact words, paper wipe and gentle mascot nod. The comparison below is retained as the decision record.

These are layout and pacing sketches using the existing draft mascot and an unchanged crop of the real logo from the original ad. The sketches have no voice or animated mascot. The finished version needs corrected character art, clean logo preparation and its selected motion. No new generation call or extra API spend was used.

## 1. Simple brand credit

**Narrator:** "Brought to you by Deep Six Pest Control."

**Screen:** "Brought to you by" above the real DEEP SIX PEST CONTROL logo.

**Picture and motion:** The lesson ends. After a brief breath, cut to a cream paper card. The logo is large and immediately readable. The original mascot stands beneath it and gives one small nod. Hold the composition at the end.

**Rough duration:** 5 seconds including the entry. A calm, matter-of-fact delivery.

**Why choose it:** The most direct wording for Drew's stated goal of making the content's source clear. This is an editorial recommendation, not a measured performance claim.

## 2. Educational sign-off

**Narrator:** "Helping you understand pests. Deep Six Pest Control."

**Screen:** The real DEEP SIX PEST CONTROL logo, with "Helping you understand pests." underneath.

**Picture and motion:** The final teaching thought finishes. A cream paper wipe leads into the matching paper setting. The mascot gives a gentle nod during the sentence break. The logo holds after the full name is spoken.

**Rough duration:** 5 seconds including the entry. A warm, conversational delivery.

**Why choose it:** Gives the series an educational purpose. Drew selected this option after seeing the comparison.

## 3. A closer look

**Narrator:** "A closer look at pests, from Deep Six Pest Control."

**Screen:** "A closer look at pests" above the real DEEP SIX PEST CONTROL logo.

**Picture and motion:** Finish the lesson. Dissolve into the paper card. The mascot gently directs its flashlight toward the already-visible logo. A soft light passes over it and settles while the brand name is spoken.

**Rough duration:** 6 seconds including the entry. A curious, friendly delivery.

**Why choose it:** Uses the mascot's investigative props to make the sign-off feel more distinctive. The motion is more involved than a small nod and may require more generation work.

## What can be mixed

The wording and visual treatment are separate choices. Any of these lines can use the simple card, paper wipe or flashlight treatment. All keep the real logo, recognizable original mascot, cream/purple/lime palette and vertical frame.

No follow, booking or website request is included in these three options. A follow request remains available in the [earlier wording list](NARRATION-OPTIONS.md) if Drew wants to trade more time for a next action.

The same selected outro will appear once in every complete narration. Its wording is now confirmed, and the narration handoff can be used. The fire-ant closing sentence will be developed only after Drew approves the finished outro, as requested.

Durations are creative pacing estimates demonstrated by silent clips, not measured voice timings or an established ideal. [Earlier research](RESEARCH.md) and [business examples](BUSINESS-EXAMPLES.md) remain supporting context.
