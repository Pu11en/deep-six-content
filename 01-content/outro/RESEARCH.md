## What the evidence supports

**Make the source visible and audible.** Google's ABCD guide supports visual and spoken branding, distinctive colors and mascots. Its evidence comes from more than 5,000 video ads and is correlational. Applying those principles to an educational sign-off is a reasoned adaptation, not a proven result for Deep Six. The same guide emphasizes early branding, so an outro alone only reaches viewers who remain to the end. [Google's ABCD reference guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/youtube_google_abcd_reference_guide_en.pdf).

**Measure the ending instead of claiming a universal best length.** YouTube explains that retention dips can indicate viewers skipping or leaving. The sources reviewed do not establish an optimal four-, five- or seven-second educational outro. Our proposed durations are creative estimates. After publication is authorized, inspect retention at the lesson-to-outro boundary. Retention alone does not measure brand recall. [YouTube retention guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en).

**Give a call to action a specific job.** Advertising guidance supports clear requests. It does not prove that every educational video needs one. For Drew's stated attribution goal, omitting a CTA is my creative recommendation. A single invitation to follow is an alternative when gaining repeat viewers becomes a priority. Avoid stacking follow, like, share, website and booking requests into this asset. [Google's ABCD reference guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/youtube_google_abcd_reference_guide_en.pdf).

**Design for the actual vertical frame.** TikTok's advertising guidance supports 9:16, readable overlays, audible speech and keeping important content clear of interface controls. These are useful production constraints, not evidence for a specific outro length or guaranteed organic performance. Keep the brand near the center, away from bottom captions and right-side controls; verify the eventual platforms before final placement. [TikTok creative guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en).

**Do not confuse an outro with a platform end screen.** YouTube's 5 to 20 second end-screen window describes its interactive overlay feature. It is not an optimum duration for a baked-in brand sign-off. YouTube documents a separate related-video link for Shorts, requiring advanced feature access. Any later link invitation must match a real available destination. [YouTube end screens](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789?hl=en), [Shorts related videos](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en).

## AEO and GEO limits

No source reviewed demonstrates that adding a branded outro increases AI citations or search rankings. Naming Deep Six clearly provides attribution to the viewer; any wider discovery benefit remains unproven.

Google says its AI search features require no special optimization beyond relevant SEO fundamentals. Its guidance includes making important information available as text. This concerns websites and Google's systems, not a promise about every answer engine. [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

For later publishing work, retain useful lesson text and accurate attribution, and use a distinct title and description for each video page. These support understandable, discoverable content; they do not establish an outro-driven ranking benefit. No publishing changes are authorized by this research. [Google video guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video).
