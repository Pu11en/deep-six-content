## Tradeoffs

- **A** gives the clearest compact source credit.
- **B** explicitly names the educational content and its source. It fits the fact-led lessons under discussion.
- **C** also fits explanations and practical lessons. It proposes an educational purpose, not a treatment or service guarantee.
- **D** gives the mascot a friendly signature, but states the source relationship less directly than A or B.
- **E** sounds like a teacher thanking a learner. It does not ask the viewer to take another action.
- **F** adds one action and extra time. The platform's actual follow or subscribe behavior must be considered before approval.

The full business name remains spoken in every option. No new tagline is presented as pre-existing Deep Six branding. The selected screen copy is now recorded in the approved specification.
