# Business examples of educational-video endings

Real businesses finish the teaching point and then add brand credit, an invitation or a short sign-off. These examples establish actual practice, not performance.

Checked 19 October 2026. Exact excerpts below come from company-published transcripts or an explicitly identified page heading. The videos were not played or assessed by ear. No view counts, conversion data, brand-recall results or AEO/GEO effects were established.

## Verified examples

## What to borrow for Deep Six

1. Complete the useful lesson before beginning the brand close.
2. Give the repeatable sign-off a clear purpose: attribution, educational identity or one next action.
3. Keep spoken copy, on-screen words and editing cues separate in the production instructions.
4. Reuse the same approved recording and visual asset; let the preceding lesson-specific sentence do the teaching.

These are creative recommendations based on the examples. They do not establish an ideal duration. Deep Six's short vertical format does not require copying the longer commercial requests in these examples.
