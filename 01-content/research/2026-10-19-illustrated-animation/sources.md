## Reproducibility limits

Snapshots preserve the exact returned text and its SHA-256. Main-branch URLs can change; observed repository heads are recorded in s15 and s19 and in the future-trial plan. API directory listings and manifests establish that files are published, not that the software runs correctly. Source code was inspected as text and never executed.
