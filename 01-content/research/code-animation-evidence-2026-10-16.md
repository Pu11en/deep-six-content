# Finished code-animation evidence

**Short science stories are the strongest fit for Deep Six.** The photon example combines an expressive character, a journey, and visual jokes. Chemistry offers a simpler diagram format.

## Scope and checks

Researched October 16, 2026. Requested window: September 16–October 16. Finished educational content in other niches, preferably made by Opus 5.5 or Astra writing animation code. The earlier tutorial watchlist missed the clarified brief.

Checked original creator posts and production descriptions, YouTube metadata, public post metadata, downloaded public clips, and representative frames. This was not full audiovisual viewing or a source-code audit. Creator claims are distinguished from observed visuals. No verified Astra example made this shortlist. No paid tools were used.

## 1. Journey of a Photon — best entertainment reference

- [Original by AstroTheWizard](https://x.com/AstroTheWizard/status/2103629247751618782): September 25, 2026, 60 seconds.
- Creator credits Opus 5.5. [Mirrored creator prompt](https://github.com/yihui-dev/awesome-opus5-5-videos/blob/main/prompts/astrothewizard-618782.md) explicitly requests JavaScript rendering, a photon character, scientific simulation, humor, and no AI video generators. This documents the requested method, not an audit of the implementation.
- Observed in sampled original frames: smiling yellow photon with a suitcase, dark purple space, a busy Sun interior, a Sun cross-section, planets, and a sunset ending.
- [Inspected frames](2026-10-16-code-animation-evidence/astrothewizard-618782-frames.jpg). [Alternative watch page](https://skillry.dev/ai-videos/opus-5-5/astrothewizard-618782) includes both the original and a separate remake; use the original as the reference.
- Adaptation: one bug makes a journey through a house; the reaper explains the hidden process. Keep a recurring palette and character design. This is a creative proposal, not a tested production template.

## 2. Chemistry explainer — best clear-diagram reference

- [Original video and production account by spdustin](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1wr268d/a_different_kind_of_opus_55_video_prompt/): Reddit displayed “18d ago” when checked; late September is confirmed, exact publication timestamp was not retrieved. Downloaded media runs 112.09 seconds.
- Creator explicitly reports Opus 5.5, JavaScript-driven HTML Canvas, no image generation, and a Chromium/FFmpeg export. Voice and music use separate AI audio services.
- Observed: dark navy background, colored atoms, clear labels, an umbrella metaphor, and a recap. [Inspected frames](2026-10-16-code-animation-evidence/chemistry-original-frames.jpg).
- Adaptation: pest life cycles, entry routes, or identification comparisons. Deep Six colors could define emphasis and categories. This appears simpler to standardize than frequent changes of visual style; that is an assessment, not a measured cost claim.
- Creator revised a confusing teaching choice. Any pest script would likewise need subject-matter review.

## 3. The Bitter Lesson — direct YouTube option

- [Finished video by Ethan Mollick](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OAwat51S_Sk): October 2, 2026; 3:08.
- [Mollick’s own production explanation](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/the-dot-and-the-swarm): Fable wrote lyrics and submitted them to Suno; Opus 5.5 handled the rest using code without image generation. Article October 1; the October 2 pinned comment links the YouTube upload.
- Teaching through a song is the transferable format. Proposed Deep Six use: a pest-myth song with repeated visual callbacks.
- Verified metadata and the creator’s account. Did not inspect the full video or establish its visual quality firsthand.

## Limits and saved proof

Most examples are individual experiments. SlopLearning provides a three-video series, but this search did not establish channel growth, audience retention, earnings, or a reliable production time. Using a format as inspiration does not establish permission to reuse its footage, music, or character assets.

The Headroom clip was excluded from the shortlist because it is a product advertisement. Examples depending on generated character images were also excluded from the main recommendations.
