## Recommended first six episodes

This order is an editorial recommendation based on service fit, homeowner usefulness, and suitability for the Photon animation format.

1. **How an ant found your kitchen.** Follow its route and end with checks for food, water, and entry points. Existing Argentine-ant references support that species; obtain fire-ant-specific material for a separate fire-ant episode.
2. **The roach hiding behind your fridge.** Follow a roach toward warmth, water, and shelter. Show useful inspection locations. Make a separate identification lesson for German versus larger outdoor roaches.
3. **The mosquito nursery hiding in your yard.** Follow development in an overlooked container and end with a yard check.
4. **How a scorpion gets into a house.** Map shelter and entry routes. Its [dedicated service page](https://www.deepsixpest.com/scorpion-control/) and local fit justify adding it; factual script research remains to be done.
5. **The mouse route you never checked.** Use a house cutaway to show door edges and utility penetrations. Focus on inspection, with repair choices matched to the opening.
6. **Flying ant or termite?** Compare visible traits and explain when to arrange an inspection.

Spiders and wasps should follow early: identification, why they gather around a property, and what residents can check. Fleas/ticks and bed bugs are confirmed services and can supply later topic-specific episodes. Pantry-pest content remains useful, but it has less explicit prominence in the reviewed Deep Six pages.
