## Selection and evidence

- Stay on the requested pest. Prior liked ant sources should help identify useful teaching approaches, not force later searches back to ants.
- Prefer original sources, identifiable expertise and a concrete homeowner question. Check the actual relevant passage or demonstration, species and Central Texas applicability. Mark dates and limitations honestly.
- Keep the useful explanation and its factual support separate from popularity. A compelling creator is not automatically right; a social question can inspire a lesson that is then checked against reliable pest evidence.
- Record the original URL, creator/publisher, platform, pest group and species, useful lesson, checked date, evidence passages or video timestamps, what was actually read/watched, limits and prior-use status. Future platform adapters must preserve this evidence; the current runner handles article/PDF cards.
- Show five numbered source links with normally one or two sentences each, maximum four, explaining what the content would teach. No hook writing in this review.
