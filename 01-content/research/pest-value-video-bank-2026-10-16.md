## Earlier animation concepts

1. **The mosquito nursery hiding in your yard.** Follow one mosquito through its early life in an overlooked container. End with a weekly check for standing water; the CDC recommends emptying and scrubbing containers. [Current CDC guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/mosquitoes/mosquito-control/mosquito-control-at-home.html).
2. **How one ant found your kitchen.** Follow an Argentine ant from outdoors to food or water, then trace its route back. End with the attractant and entry-point checks. [UC IPM ant guidance](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/ants/).
3. **The bed bug trying to hitch a ride home.** Follow a bug toward luggage. End with inspecting the sleeping area and luggage rack before unpacking. [UC IPM bed bug guidance](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/bed-bugs/).

The repeatable structure: one pest, one hidden process, one useful action. Use Deep Six’s purple, lime, black, and reaper as the guide. Keep the finished episode focused on one lesson.

## Details to preserve when writing scripts

- **Ants:** the invasion and bait videos specifically discuss Argentine ants. Do not turn their bait choices, concentrations, or timelines into claims about all ant species. The colony/worker explanation is the useful story. [UC IPM](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/ants/).
- **Bed bugs:** bites alone do not identify a bed bug problem; a quick unsuccessful inspection does not prove a room is clear. Detection devices support monitoring and do not replace treatment. [CDC identification guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/dpdx/bedbugs/).
- **Cockroaches:** a tidy home can still support roaches. Keep the lesson helpful, without blaming the homeowner. [NPIC guidance](https://npic.orst.edu/faq/roach.html). TED-Ed supplies entertaining biology; NPIC supplies practical actions.
- **Termites:** mud tubes are associated with subterranean termites, not every termite species. Preserve the difference between warning signs and a confirmed identification. [EPA guidance](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol/termites-how-identify-and-control-them).
- **Rodents:** use the company video for inspection locations. Do not imply foam alone reliably excludes rodents; durable repair methods depend on the opening. [NC State guidance](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/surveillance-and-management-of-common-structure-invading-rats).
- **Wasps:** the video uses California examples. Match the species to Deep Six’s service area before making local claims.
