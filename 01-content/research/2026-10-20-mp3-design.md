# MP3 flow design

**The canvas now continues from an approved narration to an approved MP3 that is ready for the video step.** This is Claude's recommended design. Drew likes the direction; individual rules are not formally approved yet.

## Steps added (17 to 24)

17. Pick the voice from the rotation (Tyler, Chris, Liam). The wasp episode gets Chris.
18. Prepare the read: delivery hints only. With the hints removed, the words equal the approved script.
19. Show the cost and ask Drew. One yes covers exactly one take.
20. Make one full take with a lock and a saved checkpoint.
21. Proposed: free word check with the three offline speech models the video step already uses.
22. Drew listens to a Desktop copy. No player opens.
23. Lock approval to the exact MP3 fingerprint.
24. Package the MP3, script, word timings and approvals for the video step.

## Claude's recommendations

- Run the free word check before Drew listens. It catches missing or garbled words and makes the timings the video needs.
- One full take per yes. Fix problems by re-taking the whole read, never by splicing.
- Skip voice tests for voices Drew already liked. Chris passed the hook audition.
- Keep asking before every take for now. A small standing cap per episode is Drew's later decision.
