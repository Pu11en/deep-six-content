# Deep Six: services and video priorities

**Build the series around recurring home pest problems in New Braunfels.** Deep Six’s website emphasizes ants, roaches, spiders, scorpions, and wasps. Its local FAQ calls ants the most persistent year-round problem. [Residential services](https://www.deepsixpest.com/residential-pest-control/) · [Local FAQ](https://www.deepsixpest.com/new-braunfels-pest-control-faq-what-homeowners-should-know/).

## Start with these topics

- Ants and fire ants: entry routes, colonies, and identifying the ant.
- Roaches: hiding places, moisture, and German versus outdoor roaches.
- Mosquitoes: overlooked breeding containers and yard checks.
- Spiders, wasps, scorpions: identification, shelter, and entry points.
- Rats and mice: signs and routes into the home.
- Termites: warning signs, inspections, and swarmers versus ants.

They advertise exterior-only, interior/exterior, broader monthly, and dedicated mosquito plans. Bed bugs, fleas/ticks, wildlife, commercial work, and construction-related services are also listed.
