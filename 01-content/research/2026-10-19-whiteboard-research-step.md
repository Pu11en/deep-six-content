# How the researcher finds sources

**The researcher chooses the searches and checks the lessons. Ultimate Crawler returns search results and readable page text.**

## First step for the whiteboard

1. The researcher prepares a brief: pest, Central Texas audience, useful lessons, excluded sources and research limits.
2. It sends search words to Ultimate Crawler. Real example: `site:citybugs.tamu.edu cockroaches biology behavior Texas`.
3. The crawler uses Parallel’s public web-search route. It returns titles, links and short excerpts, with up to ten results per search.
4. The researcher selects promising links and asks the crawler to read those pages.
5. The crawler returns extracted text, the final link and access details. The researcher checks support, relevance and duplicates.
6. The researcher prepares five short source previews for Drew, or reports a shortage.

## Current limits

This Deep Six route accepts Texas A&M, NPIC and EPA publications. Reddit, Facebook, X and YouTube are not connected as source-card inputs. Search snippets alone are not accepted as factual evidence. These statements describe the current route, not everything Ultimate Crawler might support.

The crawler does not run another research model. A session agent currently operates its search/read actions. No source-discovery schedule is active.
