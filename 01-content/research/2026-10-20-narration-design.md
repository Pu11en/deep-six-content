# Narration flow design

**The canvas now runs from the start of an episode to an approved narration that is ready to become an MP3.** The MP3, video and outro production stay outside this section.

## Steps added (10 to 16)

10. Plan the writing: exact hook, checked facts and limits, approved outro, narration brief, latest feedback, two approved scripts.
11. Write five full narrations, or one focused script on request. Exact hook first. One lesson. Plain spoken words. No spoken source credits.
12. A separate editing call removes repeats and filler and checks every claim.
13. Automatic checks: exact hook, exact outro once, count, duplicates, known fact IDs, banned wording.
14. Drew reads every script in chat.
15. Lock the approved words with their fingerprint, reply, time, sources and writer.
16. Move lasting corrections into the [narration brief](../narration-style.md).

## Choice points and retry routes

- Not enough checked facts: back to step 8 for only the missing research.
- A failed check: write again; a second failure stops and reports the problem to Drew.
- Drew rejects all or asks for a change: revise with the same hook and source, then return to step 10. Drew does not need to give a reason.
- The hook or source no longer fits: back to step 7.
