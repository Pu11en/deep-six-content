**Check this log before drafting every new episode.** Drew wants unique videos and no repeated content sources.

## Rules

- Log every article, video, or other source actually used for narration or factual content, including fact-check sources, as soon as a draft uses it.
- A logged content source is unavailable for another episode unless Drew explicitly approves reuse. Revisions of the same episode may keep using it.
- Record the topic and lesson too. A different URL does not make a repeated script or angle unique. Alternate URLs for the same publication count as the same source.
- Research candidates that have not supplied content remain available. Never present a drafted episode as published.
- Keep visual/style references separate. Drew selected the Photon format for reuse; that does not authorize repeating its script or another episode’s educational content.
