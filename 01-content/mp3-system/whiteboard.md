# Whiteboard: start to approved MP3

**Draw down the middle. Put the retry notes on the right.** “Crawler” means gathering sources; the writing agent checks and uses them.

```text
START: on demand / future cron
              |
              v
CONTINUE unfinished work; check approved sources
      |
      v
APPROVED SOURCE READY?
      | Yes: use it; go straight to HOOKS below.
      | No, or you requested fresh choices:
      v
PREPARE CRAWLER REQUEST
Pest, audience, useful lesson criteria,
past sources to exclude, feedback, wanted output
      |
      v
SEND REQUEST; CRAWLER searches and reads
              |
              v
CHECK evidence; show 5 short source choices
              |
              v
YOU PICK SOURCE -------- reject all: back to Crawler 1
Save every liked source; use your first choice
              |
              v
CHECK lesson depth; write 5 hooks
              |
              v
YOU PICK HOOK ---------- reject all: 5 new hooks
              |
              v
CRAWLER 2: fill gaps with supporting science
              |
              v
CHECK facts; write 5 full narrations
Each starts with your EXACT chosen hook
              |
              v
YOU PICK NARRATION ----- need deeper facts: Crawler 2
              |          wording only: rewrite
              v
KEEP WORDS; assign Tyler / Chris / Liam
              |
              v
CONFIRM AUDIO AUTHORIZATION; generate MP3
              |
              v
YOU LISTEN ------------ fix voice/tone/pronunciation
              |          then listen again
              v
APPROVE EXACT MP3; save evidence and STOP
```

**Side boxes:**

- **Source shelf:** keep all liked sources. Three or more stocked for a pest moves the next research to another pest.
- **Hook library:** separate crawler research updates opening examples. Hook writers reuse the library.
- **Voice help:** crawler checks official guidance only when needed. Audio tests still need authorization.
- **Memory:** record your choices and stated reasons. Never assume why you skipped.

Next trial skips the existing queue and starts fresh crawler research. Saved sources can enter at source selection on later requested runs. A saved hook is an alternative for its source, not permission for a second episode. Contradictory evidence pauses the flow. Word changes return to script approval.

**Used = finished MP4.** Reserve main/supporting sources while the episode is underway. Script or MP3 approval keeps that reservation; final MP4 completion permanently closes those sources, hook and script to new episodes.
