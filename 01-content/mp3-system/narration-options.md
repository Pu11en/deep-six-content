### 1

Why would a wasp lay its egg inside a cockroach’s egg case?

A small wasp called an ensign wasp puts one egg inside. When it hatches, the young wasp has a dozen or more cockroach eggs to feed on.

The young wasp uses pointed jaws with tiny teeth to cut through the shells of the individual eggs around it.

After feeding and growing, it changes into an adult inside that same case. Once that change is complete, the adult cuts a small, round hole through the case and comes out.

That adult is the wasp you might notice in your home. It’s about a quarter of an inch long, with a flag-shaped back end that bobs up and down. It doesn’t sting or bite people, so if you recognize one, you can leave it alone.
