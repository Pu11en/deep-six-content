# Learned rules

Every AI brief in `make_video.py` tells the Director, Animator, Asset maker and Reviewer to read and follow these rules. The runner adds a rule here by itself when the same problem is seen a second time; the list of problems seen so far, with where and when, is `problems-seen.json`. Edit by hand only to fix wording.

- **Small subjects too small to read.** Show insects, eggs, larvae and other small subjects in close-ups where they fill a third of the frame or more; avoid wide room shots for them. (Seen 3 times: fire-ant mound and brood, 19 Oct; ensign-wasp under-sink shots, 20 Oct. Key `subject-too-small`.)
- **Marks hidden under later drawings.** Draw highlights, lime rings and hand-held props after the thing they mark, and keep them above the caption, so nothing drawn later covers them. (Seen 2 times: fire-ant magnifier under grass and captions, 19 Oct; ensign-wasp jaw highlight under the head, 20 Oct. Key `hidden-under-later-drawing`.)
