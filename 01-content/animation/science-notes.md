## Ensign wasp and American cockroach (episode D6-01096ba333e3, October 20, 2026)

**New drawings live in `scenes/wasp-kit.mjs` and `scenes/home-world.mjs`. They follow the sources below; scale and poses are illustrative.**

### Ensign wasp, *Evania appendigaster*

- All black, small: forewing 5.5 to 7 mm ([UF/IFAS IN319](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/IN319)); body 5 to 7 mm and does not sting or bite ([TAMU City Bugs](https://citybugs.tamu.edu/2015/05/05/flag-waving-for-americans/)).
- Abdomen on a thin stalk (petiole) attached high on the propodeum; the gaster is small, flattened side to side, oval in males to nearly triangular in females (IN319). Held flag-like and waved up and down (City Bugs). Drawn as a black triangular blade on a stalk above the wings.
- Mid and hind legs widely separated; long hind legs. Long, slender antennae that tap and vibrate over the egg case (IN319, City Bugs).
- Clear wings with dark veins and a dark mark on the front edge, drawn as translucent paper.
- Egg laying: the female braces on the case and works her ovipositor through its tough wall; one white egg per case (IN319). Drawn simply: the abdomen tip bends down to the case and wriggles. The female lying on her side is not drawn.

### Larva, pupa and emergence

- Legless, pale larva. The first instar has sharply pointed jaws with a row of small teeth that cut the egg shells; later instars have different jaws (IN319). The close-up shows the first-instar jaws only.
- The larva eats all the eggs, then pupates inside the case with no cocoon (IN319). Drawn as a pale adult-shaped pupa with folded legs and wing pads that darkens to black.
- The adult cuts a small, round, jagged hole near one end of a long side of the case and leaves (IN319).

### American cockroach, *Periplaneta americana*, and its egg case

- Reddish brown with a pale brown or yellow band round the edge of the pronotum; about 4 cm; long wings over the abdomen; slender jointed cerci ([UF/IFAS EENY-141](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/IN298)). Long antennae and strong spiny legs ([UMN Extension](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/home-maintenance/household-insects/cockroaches)).
- Egg case: hardened and purse-shaped, about 8 mm long and 5 mm high, brown when laid and dark within a day or two, 16 eggs (EENY-141); two rows of eight ([Lander University](https://lanwebs.lander.edu/faculty/rsfox/invertebrates/periplaneta.html)). The ridged seam (keel) along the top follows Drew's brief and the reference photos; the sources above do not describe it.
- Left in a crack or crevice; favours very warm, moist places (UMN). The home set shows a crack, small gaps, a warm supply line and a dripping trap under a sink. No temperatures are shown.
