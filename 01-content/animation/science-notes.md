**The scene illustrates workers relocating their young; it is not a measured map of a nest.** Keep the approved narration and its evidence attached to the episode.

This follow-up used a direct public web read, not a new crawler job.
- Draw a worker with six jointed legs attached to the thorax, two small waist nodes, elbowed antennae and natural red-brown/dark coloration. Three-dimensional overlap may conceal features; do not add limbs to compensate.
- The carried larva is pale, curved and legless. The first scene can use larvae alone. If pupae appear later, use exposed developing ant shapes, consistent with [Texas A&M's fire-ant biology page](https://fireant.tamu.edu/learn/fire-ant-identification/); do not borrow the kit's cocoon.
- Do not copy the leafcutter demo's leaf cutting, fungus garden or central mound opening. See [Texas A&M's identification guidance](https://fireant.tamu.edu/manage/how-can-i-tell-if-i-have-fire-ants/).
- Position, chamber geometry and insect scale are illustrative. Do not label a fixed depth, temperature threshold or survival guarantee.
